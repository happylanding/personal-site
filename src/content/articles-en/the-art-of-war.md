# The Art of War

*The Art of War* is the earliest surviving military treatise in China and one of the oldest works on warfare in the world, predating Clausewitz's *On War* by about 2,300 years. It is honored as the "sacred classic of military science."

## Core Ideas

The book consists of thirteen chapters, roughly six thousand characters, attributed to the Spring and Autumn strategist Sun Tzu. It systematically expounds:

1. **Laying Plans** — strategic planning of war
2. **Waging War** — the economic foundation of war
3. **Attack by Stratagem** — winning through wisdom
4. **Tactical Dispositions** — the situation of attack and defense
5. **Energy** — the use of the orthodox and the unorthodox
6. **Weak Points and Strong** — avoiding strength, striking weakness

## Famous Quotes

> Know the enemy and know yourself, and you will never be defeated in a hundred battles.

> All warfare is based on deception.

> To subdue the enemy without fighting is the acme of skill.

## Modern Applications

The ideas of *The Art of War* have long transcended the military domain, finding wide application in business strategy, sports, international relations, and more. Its core principle — achieving the greatest goal at the smallest cost — remains profoundly instructive to this day.
