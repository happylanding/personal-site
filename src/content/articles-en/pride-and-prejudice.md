# Pride and Prejudice

*Pride and Prejudice* is the masterpiece of the English writer Jane Austen, published in 1813. The novel revolves around the marriage prospects of the five Bennet daughters, centered on the love story between Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy.

## Story Overview

Mrs. Bennet's greatest wish is to see all five daughters married well. When the wealthy bachelor Mr. Bingley moves into Netherfield Park, the whole town is abuzz. Accompanying Bingley is his seemingly arrogant friend — Mr. Darcy.

Elizabeth develops a deep prejudice against Darcy, and Darcy's pride does indeed wound her self-esteem. Yet through a series of misunderstandings and reversals, the two ultimately recognize each other's true hearts.

## Why It's Worth Reading

- Exquisite character development — every character feels alive
- Sharp British wit and social satire
- A timeless view of love and female independence
- Beautiful prose and intricate narrative structure

> It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife.
