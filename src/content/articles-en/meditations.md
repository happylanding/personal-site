# Meditations

*Meditations* is the personal philosophical notebook of the Roman emperor Marcus Aurelius, written between AD 170 and 180. In twelve books, it records the Stoic philosopher's reflections on life, fate, virtue, and death during his military campaigns.

## Overview of the Twelve Books

*Meditations* has no systematic philosophical system; rather, it is a collection of scattered aphorisms and reflections covering these themes:

- **Self-discipline** — how to control emotions and desires
- **Accepting fate** — aligning with the laws of nature and facing change calmly
- **Human relationships** — treating others with reason and goodwill
- **Awareness of death** — cherishing the present, unburdened by vanity

## Why It's Worth Reading

Stoic philosophy is experiencing a revival today. With its concise, sincere, and practical style, *Meditations* has become a guide to spiritual cultivation for countless people. Whether facing workplace pressure, life setbacks, or existential confusion, this book offers calm and profound insight.
