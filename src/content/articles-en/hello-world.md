## The Design Philosophy of This Site

Drawing on the minimalist design style of Li Xiaolai's personal site, this website follows these design principles:

- **Content first**: let text be the protagonist of the page and reduce visual noise
- **Timeline-driven**: articles sorted by publication date, journal-style
- **Clear navigation**: the homepage shows the latest updates, and the archive page provides complete history

## How to Use

1. Create a new `.md` file in the `src/content/articles/` directory to publish articles
2. Each article needs frontmatter metadata (title, date, tags, etc.)
3. Save and rebuild to publish
